The app is now called MSV SMART Jain Pathshala.

New in this version:
• Join Pathshala — students, shikshaks and sanchalaks can now register from inside the app and follow their request through to approval.
• Library — a rebuilt library with stavans, stotras and study material, including audio playback with a mini player.
• Team directory — meet the people behind your centre and city.
• Course progress — track section and subsection progress through each course.
• Attendance marking for Guruji is faster, with a denser roster view.
• Refreshed colours and typography throughout, and clearer Hindi text.
• More reliable sign-in, with a fresh code sent by SMS each time.

Thank you for using Jain Pathshala. Please share any feedback with us through Support.
