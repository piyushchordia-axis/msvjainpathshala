My Jain Pathshala is the official companion app for students, parents, and teachers (sanchalaks) of Jain Pathshala centres. It brings everyday Pathshala life into one simple, secure place.

For students and parents:
• Mark class attendance with on-site GPS verification
• View and submit homework
• Take online exams and quizzes
• Track progress reports and results
• Carry a digital ID card with a scannable QR code
• Submit and follow daily niyam (vows)
• Register for shivirs, competitions, and events
• Receive announcements, birthday wishes, and updates

For teachers and sanchalaks:
• Take and review attendance
• Assign homework and schedule exams
• Approve niyam submissions and registrations
• Scan ID-card QR codes for shivir attendance
• Generate progress reports and digital ID cards
• Manage forms, service requests, and announcements

Sign-in is required and is available to members of participating Jain Pathshala centres.
